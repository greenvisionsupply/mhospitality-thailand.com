About M Hospitality

We create concepts that work in real kitchens, with real teams, for real guests.

Our background

M Hospitality is a hospitality consulting firm that builds profitable food and beverage concepts for hotels, restaurants and food entrepreneurs. Our team's experience spans five-star hotels, airport lounges, high-volume kitchens and delivery-driven brands.

We combine structured workflows, realistic costing and strong brand positioning to reduce risk, improve efficiency and build profitability that lasts.

Nico Merten

Founder and advisor

Nico has more than 20 years in international hospitality, 15 of them in Thailand. His background spans five-star luxury hotels, Michelin-recognised dining, large-scale banquets, and high-volume delivery and ghost-kitchen concepts.

Career in hotels

  • Amari and Mövenpick: Sous Chef, then Head Chef.
  • Anantara Siam and Anantara Riverside: Executive Sous Chef, then Executive Chef, leading several outlets, large banquets and teams of 120+.
  • Avani Hotels: joined the opening team for pre-opening setup and launch.

Building concepts

  • Since thenHelped develop 10+ kebab shops in Thailand and supported 20+ restaurant and food concept openings.
  • TodayFounded Nico's Grill, later expanded into a cloud kitchen with several virtual brands.
